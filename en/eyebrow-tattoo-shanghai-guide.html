Eyebrow Tattoo in Shanghai: How to Choose a Studio and What to Expect

Written by the studio, for people searching in English. It explains what the methods actually are, what the recovery is, and the questions worth asking any studio in Shanghai — including ours.

1. Start with the method, not the price

"Eyebrow tattoo" covers at least three different procedures, and they heal differently:

MethodHow it is doneAwkward phaseHow long it lasts
Hand (microblading)Blade, stroke by strokeYesAbout a year
Machine (single)Pen-style device, controlled depthNo2 to 3 years
Dual machineOne machine for fuzz, one for structureNo2 to 3 years

If a studio quotes one price without asking about your brows, they are quoting a product, not a result.

2. Ask what happens to your brow shape over time

A brow is designed on a face, not on a chart. Three landmarks matter: where the brow starts (above the nostril line), where the highest point sits (above the outer edge of the iris), and where the tail ends (the line from the nostril to the outer corner of the eye). A studio should draw these on your face and let you look in a mirror before anything else happens.

3. Check who does the work

Many listings are shops with rotating staff. Ask whether the person who designs your brow is the person who tattoos it. In a one-person studio, that answer is simple.

4. Ask about the first year

Pigment settles, and a touch-up is normal. Ask whether a touch-up is included, and within what period. Ask what happens if the colour heals unevenly.

5. Look at healed work, not fresh work

Fresh brows always look good. Ask for photos taken at least a month after the procedure, ideally without makeup over them.

FAQ

What is the difference between microblading and machine brows?

Microblading is the hand method: strokes are placed with a blade, it looks more hand-drawn and has an awkward phase. Machine brows use a pen-style device for even pigment and no awkward phase. Both draw individual strokes; the tool and the healing differ.

How long does an eyebrow tattoo last in Shanghai?

Hand work lasts roughly a year before a refresh. Machine work lasts about 2 to 3 years, depending on skin type, aftercare and how much pigment the skin holds.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as scratching. Numbing is used, and the session is broken up so you can speak up at any point.

Can I go to work the next day?

With the machine methods, yes — there is no awkward phase. With the hand method, expect an awkward phase while the colour settles.

Can you cover old red or blue brows?

Sometimes. It depends on how much old pigment is there and how deep it sits. This has to be judged in person before anything is promised.
