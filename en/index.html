Eyebrow Tattoo in Shanghai — Miaomeihuamu Bespoke Aesthetics

A small private studio in Pudong, Shanghai, near Qiantan and Sanlin. We design the brow on your face before anything is tattooed, and we take two clients a day.

What we do

Three wild-brow methods, plus eyeliner, lip colour and hairline work. "Wild brow" (野生眉) is the Chinese term for a brow tattoo drawn as individual hair strokes rather than a solid shaded block.

Hand method

Strokes are placed by hand with a blade. It reads the most natural up close, and it has an awkward phase while the colour settles. Lasts roughly a year, then usually needs a refresh.

Machine method

The machine controls needle depth and speed, so pigment sits more evenly. No awkward phase — you can go back to normal the same day. Lasts about 2 to 3 years.

Dual-machine first-fuzz method

One machine lays the soft fuzz line, the other walks the main line and structure line. It is the closest to a full, natural brow for people with sparse hair.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Sparse brows, a short or missing tail, uneven brows, or brows that were tattooed before and faded to red or blue. Old pigment needs an in-person assessment first, because some cases should be corrected before anything new is added.

We will tell you if your brow foundation is a poor fit for the method you asked for. We do not upsell.

The appointment

  1. Free design, 30 to 45 minutes. Brows are drawn on your face, standing and sitting, so you can see the shape before deciding.
  2. You decide. Nothing is booked on the day unless you want to.
  3. The procedure. One client at a time, by appointment.

One free touch-up within the first year is included.

FAQ

Do you speak English?

Yes. Day-to-day communication in English is fine, and the brow design is drawn on your face so you can see it before anything starts.

Do you publish your prices?

No. Prices depend on the method and your brow foundation. Ask when you book, or use the free in-person design consultation.

Is the design free?

Yes. A brow design takes about 30 to 45 minutes, and you are not obliged to book a procedure afterwards.

How far in advance do I need to book?

The studio takes two clients a day, so booking ahead is recommended. Message or call +86 192 8218 2068.

Which method has no downtime?

The machine methods have no awkward phase. The hand method does have an awkward phase while the colour settles.

Can you fix brows done elsewhere?

Sometimes. Old red or blue pigment, or uneven brows, need an in-person assessment first — we only take the case if it is suitable.
