Facial features and bone structure before brow design

Consider the features and bone structure together. Working with that structure, the brow start, arch, tail and contour are adjusted to refine visual proportions and balance the cheekbones with the eye area.

Lele’s approach to design

Facial depth

Before designing, Lele considers the depth, visual weight and overall character of the facial features.

Visual weight and character

Consider the scale and lines of the features when choosing brow thickness and contour.

Cheekbones and facial contour

Consider cheekbone height and facial contours together. Brow position, curvature and contour help balance the visual relationship between the cheekbones and eye area.

Brow bone and eye area

Study the brow bone and the relationship between brows and eyes. Design the start, arch, tail and overall contour with respect for the existing bone structure.

Existing brows and hair direction

Study the shape, density, texture and direction of existing hairs to decide what to retain and where to add detail.

Personal character and aesthetic preferences

Soft and natural, cool and refined, or clean and strongly defined: the design responds to features, bone structure, character and preferences, rather than a fixed template.

Main strokes establish structure; fine strokes build layers

For hair-stroke designs suited to a combination of main and fine strokes, structure and hair direction come first. Finer detail then adds variations in density, tone and definition.

Main strokes
Establish the main structure and hair direction, bringing out the visual relationship between the brows and brow bone.
Fine strokes
Vary density, tone and the balance of defined and subtle lines, connecting the strokes while retaining the lightness and softness of natural hair.

The aim is more than density: a framework, layered detail and natural hair flow that complement the other features. Depth and definition between facial planes are visual effects; the design does not change bone structure.

Instrument brows and other services

Instrument hair-stroke brows are the main service. Existing brows, old pigment, skin and your preferences guide the method, rather than a fixed shape or a service tier.

All services and care information
Huapai single-instrument hair-stroke brows

One instrument is selected for the existing hair texture. Main and supporting strokes refine the outline and hair flow. A possible choice for subtle definition that works with existing brows; suitability depends on the individual design.

Huapai dual-instrument layered hair-stroke brows

Two instruments combine main structural strokes with fine strokes. Where this combination is suitable, main strokes establish structure and hair direction, bringing out the visual relationship between brows and brow bone. Fine strokes vary density, tone and definition for natural transitions and the lightness of existing hair. Suitability depends on the individual’s brows and style; the aim is not density alone.

Manual hair-stroke brows

Strokes are placed with a manual blade, with pressure and direction controlled by the practitioner. Often considered when there is an established natural brow framework. Colour may look darker during the first week; about seven days is an experience-based recovery guide, not a guaranteed schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only design soft, natural-looking brows?

No. Lele personally favours soft, natural brows and the restraint of Eastern aesthetics. Bespoke design does not mean one style for everyone: soft and natural, cool and refined, or clean and strongly defined brows can all be considered in relation to your features, bone structure, character and preferences.

Is the design consultation free?

Yes. The design consultation is free and usually takes 30–45 minutes. Discuss your facial features, bone structure, personal character and aesthetic preferences, see the proposed shape, then decide whether to proceed. There is no charge if you decide not to.

Where can I check prices and touch-up terms?

Check the studio’s current Dianping listing for prices. All services include free touch-ups within one year. The service and aftercare plan are confirmed before treatment.

Can recovery time or pigment retention be guaranteed?

No. Skin, existing pigment, the procedure and aftercare can affect healing and retention. Any time estimate is an experience-based guide, not a guaranteed result.
