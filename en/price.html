Available services

All services include free touch-ups within one year. The design consultation is free and takes about 30–45 minutes, even if you decide not to proceed.

Huapai single-instrument hair-stroke brows

One instrument is selected for the existing hair texture. Main and supporting strokes refine the outline and hair flow. A possible choice for subtle definition that works with existing brows; suitability depends on the individual design.

Huapai dual-instrument layered hair-stroke brows

Two instruments combine structural strokes with finer, softer strokes. Main strokes establish the shape and primary hair flow; fine strokes add internal layers, density and softer transitions. The aim is coherent structure, not simply darker brows. Sparse hair alone does not determine the choice.

Manual hair-stroke brows

Strokes are placed with a manual blade, with pressure and direction controlled by the practitioner. Often considered when there is an established natural brow framework. Colour may look darker during the first week; about seven days is an experience-based recovery guide, not a guaranteed schedule.

Silk-shaded brows (丝雾眉)

An available brow service. Discuss the existing brows, underlying pigment and desired definition in person before choosing a technique.

Velvet-shaded brows (绒雾眉)

An available brow service. First discuss the preferred contour and colour intensity, then assess whether it suits your brows.

Lash-line enhancement

Definition along the lash line. Black gives a stronger visual presence, while brown is softer. Suitability and aftercare are discussed before treatment.

Lip-contour refinement

Refines the visual shape and edges of the lips. It does not add tissue volume and is distinct from full-lip colour correction. Surface flaking can occur; about three days is an experience-based guide, with individual variation.

Dark-lip colour correction

Assessment-led colour correction for darker lips, with contour refinement where appropriate. Surface flaking can occur. A recovery plan is discussed after assessment rather than assigning the same timeline to everyone.

Hairline pigmentation

Hair-like lines follow the existing direction to address some visible gaps or faint edges. Pigmentation does not regrow hair. Large areas of hair loss or substantial repositioning need a different assessment.

Philtrum shading

A localised pigmentation service for the philtrum area. Discuss the intended visual detail and suitability first; it does not change bone or tissue structure.

All services include free touch-ups within one year. For prices, see the current Dianping listing.

Healing and retention: estimates, not guarantees.

Retention is commonly discussed as around one year for manual brows and two to three for instrument brows. Manual work may appear darker during the first week. Lip-contour and dark-lip work may involve surface flaking; no fixed recovery period is assigned to dark-lip correction.

Skin, existing pigment, the procedure and aftercare can affect the outcome. Confirm washing, makeup, swimming and exercise arrangements for the specific service and actual healing.

Questions before a visit

Is the design consultation free?

Yes. The design consultation is free and usually takes 30–45 minutes. Discuss your brows and preferences, see the proposed shape, then decide whether to proceed. There is no charge if you decide not to.

Where can I check prices and touch-up terms?

Check the studio’s current Dianping listing for prices. All services include free touch-ups within one year. The service and aftercare plan are confirmed before treatment.

Can recovery time or pigment retention be guaranteed?

No. Skin, existing pigment, the procedure and aftercare can affect healing and retention. Any time estimate is an experience-based guide, not a guaranteed result.

Can existing brow pigment be worked over?

The depth, extent and shape of existing pigment must be assessed first. Darker residual pigment may need advice on removal from an appropriately qualified provider. This studio does not offer pigment removal.
