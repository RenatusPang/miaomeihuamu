Three methods.
One individual starting point.

Tools and stroke arrangements differ. The design still begins with facial features, existing brows and individual preference.

Huapai single-instrument hair-stroke brows

One instrument is selected for the existing hair texture. Main and supporting strokes refine the outline and hair flow. A possible choice for subtle definition that works with existing brows; suitability depends on the individual design.

Huapai dual-instrument layered hair-stroke brows

Two instruments combine structural strokes with finer, softer strokes. Main strokes establish the shape and primary hair flow; fine strokes add internal layers, density and softer transitions. The aim is coherent structure, not simply darker brows. Sparse hair alone does not determine the choice.

Manual hair-stroke brows

Strokes are placed with a manual blade, with pressure and direction controlled by the practitioner. Often considered when there is an established natural brow framework. Colour may look darker during the first week; about seven days is an experience-based recovery guide, not a guaranteed schedule.

Questions before a visit

Do you only design soft, natural-looking brows?

No. Lele personally favours a soft, natural look, but each design responds to the facial features, existing brows and the character the client wants. Her work is not limited to one brow style.

Can recovery time or pigment retention be guaranteed?

No. Skin, existing pigment, the procedure and aftercare can affect healing and retention. Any time estimate is an experience-based guide, not a guaranteed result.

Can existing brow pigment be worked over?

The depth, extent and shape of existing pigment must be assessed first. Darker residual pigment may need advice on removal from an appropriately qualified provider. This studio does not offer pigment removal.
