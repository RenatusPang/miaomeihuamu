Brow design, healing and touch-ups: common questions

Clients often ask how design choices, recovery phases and touch-up policies interact. Here are direct answers separating each topic.

1. How are brow thickness and arch position decided?

Three factors guide width and arch position: eye-to-brow proportion and eye scale, the natural turning point along the brow ridge and cheekbones, and how often you wear bare skin versus light makeup.

2. Are denser, darker brows better value?

Quite the opposite. Natural hair-stroke appeal relies on skin breathing between individual lines and staggered density. Over-packing strokes makes brows look stamped early on and blurs spacing as pigment gradually fades.

3. Does pigment fade cleanly or turn red and blue? How should retention be viewed?

Our approach allows pigment to metabolise gradually without building stubborn red, blue or grey undertones. Speed varies by skin—oily skin tends to fade faster than drier skin. Working estimates are about one year for manual brows and two to three years for instrument brows, always varying individually.

4. Which services allow washing the same day, and what about aftercare?

Same-day washing applies only to Huapai single-instrument brows, dual-instrument brows, lash-line enhancement and hairline pigmentation. Manual brows (~7 days guide, darker week 1), shaded brows, lip contour (~3 days scabbing guide), dark-lip correction and philtrum shading require tailored healing care with the provided aftercare kit.

5. How do the free consultation and 1-year touch-up policy work?

The 30–45 minute trial-design consultation is free with zero obligation and zero upselling. Current pricing is published on our Dianping listing and confirmed before treatment. Every service includes free touch-ups within one year once initial healing settles.
