Lip pigmentation: recovery and everyday care

Both lip-contour and dark-lip work progress through initial saturation, surface flaking and gradual tone settling. Follow service-specific instructions rather than copying someone else’s day-by-day calendar.

Why colour looks stronger initially and how to treat flaking skin

Fresh pigment plus initial dryness can make lips appear darker than their settled tone. As flaking begins—roughly three days as a guide for contour work, and individually variable for dark-lip correction—never pull or peel loose skin; letting it shed naturally with gentle moisture prevents patchy colour loss.

Hydration, meals, cleansing and returning to lipstick

Because lips face frequent contact with food and water, follow your aftercare ointment and gentle cleansing routine closely. Avoid piping-hot, very spicy or overly salty meals early on, and confirm with Lele when everyday lipstick or heavy workouts fit your actual healing stage.

Tone shifts after flaking: why day-four photos do not tell the whole story

Right after flaking finishes, lips often pass through a transitional muted phase—especially after dark-lip neutralisation—before true undertone balance becomes clear. Allow the full settling period before judging colour evenness.

Review, one-year touch-up and medical precautions

All lip services include free touch-ups within one year once settled tone can be evaluated accurately. If persistent swelling, marked discomfort or unusual reactions appear during recovery, contact us immediately and consult a doctor if needed.
