Can very sparse brows be designed?

Suitability and achievable detail depend on the starting point. Hair-stroke pigmentation creates a visual effect with lines and colour; it does not grow new hairs.

Identify what is missing

An intact but faint brow differs from an almost absent brow, a local gap or a missing tail. Observe existing hairs and pigment before discussing the outline.

Plan structure and density separately

Where a combination of main and fine strokes is appropriate, main strokes establish structure and hair direction, bringing out the visual relationship between brows and brow bone. Fine strokes then vary density, tone and definition to connect the lines naturally. The aim is a framework with layers and natural hair flow that complements the features, not filling every gap or simply adding darkness.
