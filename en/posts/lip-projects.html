Lip-contour refinement and colour correction

Lip outline and natural lip colour are different concerns. Start by identifying the detail you want to address.

Lip-contour refinement

This focuses on the lip edge, visual balance between upper and lower lips and local contour. It does not add tissue volume or recolour the entire lip. The studio uses an instrument for this service.

Dark-lip colour correction

This involves assessing the natural lip colour, colour correction and any appropriate contour refinement. Suitability and staging need an in-person assessment; a specific colour in one session is not guaranteed.

Different aftercare discussions

Both services may involve surface flaking. Around three days is an experience-based guide for contour work; no fixed recovery period is assigned to dark-lip correction. Follow the individual aftercare plan. All services include free touch-ups within one year.
