Lip-contour refinement and colour correction

Many enquiries mix up wanting a clearer lip silhouette with wanting to neutralise dark, cool lip undertones. Contour refinement and dark-lip colour correction tackle two separate issues.

Lip-contour refinement: subtle edge framing without volume filler

Lip-contour refinement targets blurred borders, flat Cupid’s bows or upper/lower proportion balance using an electric instrument with conservative placement along the natural margin. It refines visual shape and does not add physical tissue volume like injectable fillers.

Dark-lip correction: warm complementary neutralisation before soft layering

Applying bright red or pink straight onto naturally dark, cool or uneven lips leaves underlying grey-purple tones showing through, resulting in a muddy healed shade. Proper dark-lip correction first balances cool undertones with warm complementary pigment before layering translucent colour smoothly.

Staging deep undertones and optional pairing with philtrum shading

Mild dullness along the border differs from deep all-over dark undertones, so whether staging is sensible must be judged in natural light rather than promising an exact lipstick shade in one session. Clients wanting subtle mid-face proportion balance can also explore philtrum shading alongside contour work.

Different healing timelines: both involve scabbing, not the same day count

Unlike instrument brow work, both lip services involve a scabbing phase. Contour refinement typically uses around three days as an experience guide, whereas dark-lip correction is never assigned a single universal day count due to wide individual variation. Both include free touch-ups within one year.
