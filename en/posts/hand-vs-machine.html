Manual and single-instrument brows

Tools change how strokes are placed. Existing brows, the design and the practitioner’s technique still determine suitability.

Manual strokes

Individual strokes are placed with a manual blade. Pressure and direction are controlled by the practitioner. This may be considered when the natural framework is relatively complete and the brief is to refine the outline or add hair-like detail.

Selecting one instrument

The texture and colour of existing hair inform instrument choice. The number of instruments does not replace decisions about shape, spacing and direction.

Discuss healing and retention separately

Manual work may look darker during the first week; around seven days is a recovery guide. Instrument work also requires an individual aftercare plan. Retention is commonly discussed as about one year for manual work and two to three for instrument work, with individual variation.
