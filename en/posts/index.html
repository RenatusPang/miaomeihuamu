Thoughtful questions.
Practical explanations.

Design, hair direction, choosing a service and everyday care. One topic at a time, grounded in the individual rather than a service label.

From main strokes to fine layers: nine design steps

These existing illustrations explain a drawing sequence. They are not client cases or instructions for carrying out a pigmentation procedure. The design still needs to respond to the individual’s structure, existing brows and preferences.

↗

Choosing a brow studio: from work records to consultation

Review the work carefully and ask how the design and procedure will be handled. These practical checks can help when choosing a studio.

↗

Lash-line enhancement: black or brown?

Consider the eye area, everyday makeup and desired definition before choosing a colour. Darker is not automatically more suitable.

↗

Brow design, healing and touch-ups: common questions

Clarify design, suitability and aftercare before deciding. These questions separate issues that are often discussed as if they were the same thing.

↗

What to observe during the first month

Colour and surface appearance may change in stages. This is a way to observe recovery, not a fixed calendar that applies to everyone.

↗

What hairline pigmentation can and cannot do

Hairline pigmentation uses hair-like lines to address some visible gaps and edges. It is different from hair transplantation.

↗

Manual and single-instrument brows

Tools change how strokes are placed. Existing brows, the design and the practitioner’s technique still determine suitability.

↗

How long do hair-stroke brows last?

Semi-permanent pigment changes over time. Retention can only be estimated in context; a service name cannot guarantee a duration.

↗

Single or dual instruments: choosing a brow method

Start with the existing brows and the desired character. The difference is how strokes and structure are combined, rather than a simple hierarchy of devices.

↗

Lip-contour refinement and colour correction

Lip outline and natural lip colour are different concerns. Start by identifying the detail you want to address.

↗

Lip pigmentation: recovery and everyday care

Healing varies with the service and the person. A set number of days is not a guarantee that recovery is complete.

↗

Working with old brow pigment

Before redesigning, assess the depth, extent and shape of old pigment. Adding new colour does not automatically cover what is already there.

↗

Can very sparse brows be designed?

Suitability and achievable detail depend on the starting point. Hair-stroke pigmentation creates a visual effect with lines and colour; it does not grow new hairs.

↗

Sparse brows, missing tails and local gaps

Brows that all look sparse can have different needs. Separate questions of density, framework and hair direction.

↗

Miaomeihuamu: a private studio for individual design

Miaomeihuamu is a private cosmetic-pigmentation studio centred on personal aesthetic judgement and individual brow design, with instrument hair-stroke brows as its main service.

↗

Choosing a brow studio in Pudong

Look beyond service names. Real work, the practitioner, the design process and clear service terms provide a more useful basis for a decision.

↗

Who designs your brows, and who performs the work?

The same tools can be used with different design and technical approaches. Review the work, design reasoning and the person who will perform the procedure together.

↗

Choosing a brow method for your starting point

A service is a way to carry out a design. Start with the existing brows, facial proportions and personal preferences before choosing the method.

↗

Three approaches to hair-stroke brows

Hair-stroke brows use individual lines to suggest natural hair direction. The same broad name can describe different tools and combinations of strokes.
