Working with old brow pigment

Before redesigning, assess the depth, extent and shape of old pigment. Adding new colour does not automatically cover what is already there.

Colour and shape constraints

The width, tail position and extent of old pigment can restrict a new shape. Red, blue or grey residual colour can have several contributing factors, including pigment and placement. The specific situation needs assessment.

When removal advice may be needed

If dark residual pigment limits redesign, seek removal advice from an appropriately qualified provider. The studio does not offer pigment removal, and old-brow correction is not its main service.

An in-person decision

Even faint old pigment is not an automatic indication for new work. Identify what can be retained, which contours are constrained and whether stages may be needed before selecting a technique.
