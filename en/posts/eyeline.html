Lash-line enhancement: black or brown?

Lash-line enhancement is more than picking black or brown ink. Eye shape, eyelid crease width and outer-corner angle decide both pigment shade and line thickness.

Black vs brown: matching bare-skin habits and iris tone

Black creates stronger lash-root contrast, often chosen by clients with dark irises who enjoy clear eye framing. Brown—one of our available colour options—gives a gentler blend along the roots that suits frequent bare-skin wear, lighter hair or delicate lids.

Four eye-shape observations checked during consultation

During assessment, Lele inspects eye shape and scale, lid-crease width (hooded, narrow or wider double eyelids), outer-corner tilt and left-right balance. Where more sclera shows, placing a fine line cleanly along the inner lash margin helps frame the eye visually, while subtle thickness tuning can help balance perceived asymmetry.

Subtle lash-line framing without exaggerated wings

Lash-line work focuses cleanly on the root zone—refined with eyes open and understated when closed—avoiding thick, trendy winged extensions that age awkwardly. Aesthetic goals vary individually and are never framed as medical claims or uniform guarantees.

No awkward recovery phase, same-day washing and one-year touch-up

As an instrument service, lash-line enhancement avoids an awkward heavy-flaking phase and generally allows normal face washing the same day, with tailored care notes confirmed at your visit. Colour options and free touch-ups within one year are included.
