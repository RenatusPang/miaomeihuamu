Lele.
Brows considered with the whole face.

A soft, natural appearance is Lele’s personal preference, not a fixed answer for every client. Each design begins with the individual and their preferences.

From professional experience
to a private studio.

Lele entered the field in 2016, worked as an external practitioner for a large beauty chain and within a specialist brow chain, and gained training and teaching experience.

In August 2024 she opened Miaomeihuamu in Sanlin, Pudong. She now personally handles design and treatment, seeing one client at a time.

The details that
guide a design.

Work with the existing structure, relating the brow start, arch and tail to facial proportions. Lele favours a natural, soft look personally; each design still responds to your preferences.

Lele’s approach to design

Facial depth

Look at the relationship between the eyes, nose and lips before deciding how much definition the brows need.

Visual weight and character

Consider the scale and lines of the features when choosing brow thickness and contour.

Cheekbones and facial contour

Consider cheekbone prominence and facial contours when placing the arch and tail.

Brow bone and eye area

Work with the existing bone structure, brow-to-eye distance and proportions from different angles.

Existing brows and hair direction

Study the shape, density, texture and direction of existing hairs to decide what to retain and where to add detail.

The character you want

Understated, soft, defined or more expressive: your preferences are part of the brief.

Structure, with softer layers.

Where a combination of structural and fine strokes is appropriate, establish the framework before planning internal density and transitions.

Main strokes
Establish the framework, structure and primary hair direction.
Finer strokes
Add internal layers, density and softer transitions.

Tone, spacing and direction contribute to visual depth. The aim is not simply darker brows, and pigmentation does not change bone structure. Results vary by person.

Your preferences belong in the design.

Whether you prefer softness, definition or a stronger brow presence, say so. The design works with existing bone structure, adjusting the start, arch, tail and contour to seek visual flow, balanced proportions and depth.

These are design aims, not guaranteed outcomes or changes to actual bone structure.
