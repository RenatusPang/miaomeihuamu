Lele’s brow design philosophy

Grounded in each person’s bone structure, main strokes establish structure and fine strokes enrich the layers. Individual brow design brings the brows into harmony with the other features, seeking a more fluid, dimensional and natural overall appearance.

Lele’s professional background

Lele entered the field in 2016, worked as an external practitioner for a large beauty chain and within a specialist brow chain, and gained training and teaching experience.

In August 2024 she opened Miaomeihuamu in Sanlin, Pudong. She now personally handles design and treatment, seeing one client at a time.

Brow design grounded in bone structure and facial harmony

Lele’s brow design always begins with each person’s own features and bone structure, rather than a fixed brow template.

Lele’s approach to design

Before designing, she considers the depth, visual weight and overall character of the features, the height and prominence of the cheekbones and brow bone, the relationship between brows and eyes, and the direction of the existing brow hairs.

Working with the existing bone structure, she adjusts the position and curvature of the brow start, arch and tail, along with the overall contour, to refine facial proportions visually and balance the relationship between the cheekbones and eye area. The aim is a smoother, more open visual flow, with natural depth and definition between facial planes.

Main strokes establish structure; fine strokes build layers

Lele pays close attention to the framework, structure and layers of hair flow within the brows.

Main strokes
In hair-stroke brow designs suited to a combination of main and fine strokes, the main strokes first establish the primary structure and direction of hair flow, bringing out the visual relationship between the brows and brow bone.
Fine strokes
Delicate, fine strokes then introduce variations in density, tone, and the balance of defined and subtle lines. The strokes connect naturally, giving the brow a clear contour and structure while retaining the lightness and softness of natural hair.

The result sought is more than density alone: a brow with a framework, layered detail and natural hair flow that complements the other facial features.

A preference for softness, with room for every individual style

Lele personally favours soft, natural brows that retain the character of the original hair, with the softness and restraint of Eastern aesthetics.

But a bespoke service does not mean giving every client the same brows.

Whether you prefer soft and natural brows, a cool and refined look, or clean, strong definition, the design can be tailored to your features, bone structure, personal character and aesthetic preferences.
